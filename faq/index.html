Is joining a bundle free?

Yes. Households pay nothing to start or join a bundle. You only pay for the service itself, and only after you approve the winning price and the crew finishes your home.

When am I charged?

When you approve the winning price, your card is authorized (held), not charged. The charge goes through only after the pro marks your route complete. If the route never runs, the hold is released.

What does my bundle see about me?

Your first name and street. Your full address goes only to the winning pro, after you approve the price.

How does bidding open?

Automatically. The moment a bundle reaches its home count, verified local pros in that service and ZIP get an alert and a 48-hour window to bid on the whole route.

How is the winner picked?

The lowest qualified bid when bidding closes. Ties go to the higher-rated pro. Flexible-timing bundles compare prices after each pro's flex discount.

What if no pro bids?

We extend bidding 24 hours once. If nobody bids, the bundle closes and you're not charged anything.

How are pros vetted?

Pros apply with their business details, the services they offer, their service ZIPs and license info. We verify license and insurance before a pro can bid, and every finished bundle rates the crew.

What do pros pay?

A small fee, 8%, only on bundles they win and complete. No lead fees, no subscriptions, no paying for clicks that never turn into work.

Can my AI assistant join bundles for me?

Yes, with your OK. Create a key in your account. Your assistant can read open bundles and ask to join one. Nothing happens until you approve the request.

Where does StreetBundle work?

StreetBundle is headquartered in Denver and bundles can form anywhere in the US. A bundle needs a ZIP code, a need and enough neighbors.

What services can a bundle buy?

Recurring and seasonal home services that are the same from house to house: gutters, roof checks, water heaters, HVAC tune-ups, window washing, pest control, tree trimming, dryer vents, chimney sweeps, pressure washing, aeration, holiday lights, sprinkler blowouts and solar panel cleaning.

Can I leave a bundle?

Yes, anytime while the bundle is still filling. Once bidding opens your spot counts toward the route, and after the winner is picked you can simply not approve the price.
