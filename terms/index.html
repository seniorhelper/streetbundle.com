The service

StreetBundle organizes groups of households and lets independent, verified pros bid to serve them. Pros, not StreetBundle, perform the work.

Bundles and approvals

Joining is free and non-binding. Approving a winning price authorizes a card hold. The charge is captured after the pro marks your route complete.

Cancellations

You can leave while a bundle is filling. After approval, contact us before the service date to cancel and release your hold.

Disputes

If work isn't done as bid, report it within 7 days of completion and we'll work it out with the pro.

Conduct

No fake bundles, fake reviews or harassment of neighbors or pros.
