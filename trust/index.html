Trust & safety

Safe by design.

Verified pros. Pay after the work. Your address stays private.

Verified before bidding

Pros list their services, service ZIPs and license. We verify license and insurance before a pro can bid. Pros who miss dates or slip on quality lose access.

Holds, not charges

Approving a price authorizes your card. It's captured only after the pro marks your route complete. If the route doesn't run, the hold is released.

Privacy by default

Neighbors see first names and street. The winning pro gets addresses only after approval. AI assistants can only ask, never act.

Every bundle rates

Each home rates the crew after the route. Ratings break ties in bidding, so good work wins the next street.
