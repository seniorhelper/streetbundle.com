How pros win

From alert to paid in five steps.

1

Get verified

Apply with your services, service ZIPs and license. We confirm license and insurance before you can bid.

2

Get the alert

When a bundle fills in your area, you're notified by text and email with homes, ZIP and window.

3

Bid the route

Set a per-home price, an optional flex discount and a service date. The bid tool shows your route revenue after fees.

4

Work the street

Win, and the route locks once homes approve. You see every stop, then start and complete the route from your phone.

5

Get paid, get rated

Marking complete captures every card hold and pays out to your connected account. Ratings build your edge on the next bundle.

Why the math works.

Field crews can lose 10–15% of productive capacity to windshield time between scattered jobs. A bundle puts five to twenty homes on one street, so the drive time you save can fund the lower price.
