What we believe

Buying alone is the weakest position in any market. Neighbors already share fences, streets and storms. Pooling the same need is the oldest form of buying power, and it has been too much work to organize. StreetBundle makes it a tap.

Local pros are the other half. A good crew stuck in traffic between scattered jobs is wasted skill. A bundle gives them a full street and a fair price.

And when neighbors stand on the same sidewalk watching the same crew, they meet. That might be the best part.
