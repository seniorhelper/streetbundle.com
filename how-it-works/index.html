The playbook

One need. One street. One price.

Every bundle follows the same rules, and the triggers run on their own.

Start a bundle☎ 1-800-481-8638
1

Declare a need

Gutters, a roof check, a water heater on its last year. Pick a window, or go flexible for a better price.

2

Neighbors join

Share the link in your street's group text. Each home fills a square on the bundle meter.

3

Bidding opens on its own

The moment the bundle hits its number, verified local pros get 48 hours to bid on the whole route.

4

Approve, hold, done

Lowest qualified bid wins. You approve, your card is held, the crew works the street, then you pay and rate.

The rules, exactly

What happens, and when.

TriggerWhat happens automatically
Bundle startedThe starter is home #1. The bundle stays open 21 days.
Halfway fullEveryone in the bundle gets a nudge to share it with one more neighbor.
One spot leftMembers get an alert: one more home opens bidding.
Home count reachedStatus flips to Bidding. Verified pros with that service and ZIP get an alert and a 48-hour window.
New best bidMembers see the new price per home. The pro who was leading gets an outbid alert.
Bidding closesLowest qualified bid wins (after flex discount on flexible bundles). Ties go to the higher-rated pro.
No bidsBidding extends 24 hours once. Still nothing? The bundle closes and nobody is charged.
Winner pickedEach home has 72 hours to approve. Approving places a card hold, not a charge.
Approvals inThe route locks and the pro sees every stop. Homes that didn't approve drop off.
Route completeHolds are captured, the pro is paid, and every home is asked to rate the crew.
21 days, not fullThe bundle expires. Start again anytime, or join a bigger one nearby.

Flexible timing

Homes that let the pro choose the day get the pro's flex discount. Pros build tighter routes, you pay less.

Privacy by default

Neighbors see your first name and street. The winning pro gets your address only after you approve.

Pay after the work

Approving holds your card. The charge goes through when the crew finishes. No route, no charge.
